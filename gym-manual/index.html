PROGRAM RULES

How to run the plan

ProgressionUse double progression. When every working set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form, add the smallest sensible amount of load and restart near the lower end.
EffortMost compounds: ~2 RIR. Isolation: usually 1–2 RIR. Smith RDL and split squat: do not chase failure.
RestLarge compounds 2–3 min; presses/rows/pulldowns ~2 min; isolation 60–90 sec.
Pain ruleMuscular effort/burn is expected. Sharp joint pain, nerve-like pain, or worsening back pain means stop that movement and use an alternative.
HamstringsDynamic mobility before training. Longer static hamstring stretching after training/rest days. On RDLs, useful range matters more than reaching the floor.
ConditioningWeights come first because muscle gain is the priority. Keep the finisher short and technically simple; it should improve work capacity without wrecking recovery.
DeloadIf performance drops across several sessions, soreness stays high, sleep worsens, or motivation collapses, reduce working sets ~40–50% for one week.
CURRENT BASELINE

Nutrition & recovery

Current estimateTDEE ≈ 3,070 kcal/day; slow-gain starting target ≈ 3,350 kcal/day. Calibrate with your bodyweight and waist trend rather than force-feeding.
ProteinKeep protein high and spread across the day. Prioritize whole-food protein, carbs around training, fruit/veg and adequate fats.
Rate of gainAim for slow scale increase. If weight is flat for 2–3 weeks and gym performance is not improving, add a small amount of food. If waist rises quickly, reduce the surplus.
RecoverySleep 7–8+ hours when possible. Walking and light mobility on non-training days are useful; avoid adding random hard cardio.
