Program rules

How to run it

Progression
When all working sets reach the top of the rep range with clean form, add the smallest sensible load and restart near the bottom of the range.
Effort
Most compounds ~2 reps in reserve. Isolation 1–2 RIR. Never chase failure on Smith RDL or split squat.
Rest
Large compounds 2–3 min; presses/rows/pulldowns about 2 min; isolation 60–90 sec.
Hamstrings
Dynamic mobility before lifting; longer static stretching after training. On RDLs stop at your useful hamstring range, not at an arbitrary floor depth.
Conditioning
Weights first. Keep finishers short, low-skill and technically clean. They should improve work capacity, not destroy recovery.
Pain
Muscle burn is fine. Sharp joint pain, nerve-like pain, or worsening back pain means stop and use an alternative.
